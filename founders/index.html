What you can practise

Three tracks. One ladder built for you.

Tell us where you get stuck and we build your modules from these three tracks. Most founders need a mix.

Track 1

Public speaking

For the pitch, the stage, the podcast and the camera. Rehearse the real thing in private until nerves stop running it.

  • You practise: the camera pitch, hooks and openings, client stories, Q&A and objections.
  • You leave able to: stand up and deliver a pitch or talk without a script in your hand.
Track 2

Articulation

For founders who get asked to repeat themselves, speak too fast, or lean on "um" and "basically".

  • You practise: breath and pace, clear consonants, stress, intonation that sounds sure, no fillers.
  • You leave able to: be understood the first time, at a calm 120 to 160 words a minute.
Track 3

Subject-matter expertise

For experts who know too much and say all of it. Modules are written around your own subject and material.

  • You practise: point, reason, example, point. Explaining your field in 60 seconds. Answering hard questions briefly.
  • You leave able to: explain what you know so a buyer or investor gets it at once.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is Founder Rehearsal?

Founder Rehearsal is a private speaking practice platform for founders, customised to each founder's needs. Each member gets their own login and a ladder of short modules built around one goal, such as public speaking, articulation, or explaining their expertise. They practise for about ten minutes a day, record each attempt, and move to the next module only when they have passed the current one.

Who is Founder Rehearsal for?

It is for busy founders and business owners who avoid speaking, feel shy on camera or on stage, ramble when explaining their product, or have a pitch, talk or interview coming up. It also suits founders who speak English as a second language and want to sound clearer.

What can I practise?

There are three tracks. Public speaking covers pitches, talks, camera work and Q&A. Articulation covers pace, clear consonants, stress, intonation and cutting filler words. Subject-matter expertise covers explaining what you know clearly and briefly. Your ladder can combine all three.

How much time does it take?

About ten minutes a day. Modules are made of short drills, so you can finish a session before your first meeting.

Do I have to speak in front of anyone?

No. You practise alone in your own space. There is no audience, no group call and no leaderboard.

How is it different from a public speaking course or a coach?

A course teaches everyone the same content, and a coach costs hundreds per hour and depends on your calendar. Founder Rehearsal gives you modules written around your own goal and material, a daily practice habit, and recordings that let you hear your own progress.

How do I see my progress?

Every take is saved in your space. You can play your first recording next to your latest, and see which modules you have passed.

What does it cost?

The price depends on the modules built around your needs. Send us your application and we will get in touch, agree your goal and modules, and give you your price before you commit to anything.

How do I join?

Fill in the application form with the one thing you want to fix. We reply personally to arrange a short call and build your first modules.
