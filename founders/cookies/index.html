Cookies and browser storage

Founder Rehearsal · Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity on our public pages only if you press Accept. If you press Reject, or make no choice, we do not load either. We do not ask either service to store data for advertising (Microsoft explains its own use below), and there is no analytics in the member room.

What is stored

WhatWhere and whenWhy
Your analytics choice (browser local storage, named fr-analytics-2; an older item may be named fr-analytics)Public pages, after you press Accept or Reject. We ask again after about 6 monthsStrictly necessary to remember your choice
Analytics cookies named _ga and _ga_EEWT6MLNNYOnly if you accept. Set to expire after 60 days without a further visitGoogle Analytics uses them to count visits and tell browsers apart. Optional
Clarity cookies named _clck and _clsk, and short-lived items in browser storageOnly if you acceptMicrosoft Clarity uses them to recognise your browser and join the pages of one visit. Optional
Microsoft’s own cookies, such as CLID and MUIDMay be set on Microsoft’s domains once Clarity loadsMicrosoft says it uses them for purposes including advertising, site analytics and operations. Optional
Sign-in session (browser local storage), and a temporary sign-in check while you are signing inThe member room, when you sign in. The session is removed when you sign outStrictly necessary to sign you in and keep you signed in
Security cookies, if usedOur hosting and sign-in providers may set them to protect the serviceStrictly necessary for security

If you accept, Google receives information such as the pages you view, your device and browser, how you arrived and your approximate location. We also record that an application was sent, without anything you typed. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Microsoft Clarity records how a visit moves through a page, including mouse movements, clicks, taps and scrolling, with page text and typed content masked. Microsoft handles this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement, and says it uses Clarity data to improve its products and for advertising.

Change your choice

Select “Cookie settings” at the bottom of a public page at any time. Pressing Reject stops further analytics from this site on this browser and removes the analytics cookies this site can reach. Cookies on Microsoft’s own domains can be removed in your browser settings. It does not delete statistics already collected; email us about those.

Other services

If you choose “Continue with Google”, you visit Google’s pages, where Google uses its own cookies under its cookie policy. The member room also loads a code file from jsDelivr, and scored practice uses your browser’s speech service. Our privacy policy explains both. Links in our guides take you to other websites with their own rules.

How to remove it

Sign out of your room, or clear site data for founderrehearsal.com in your browser settings. If you block this storage, signing in may not work. The public pages can still be read.

If this changes

If we add anything else that is not strictly necessary, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Questions: hello@founderrehearsal.com.

Common questions

Does Founder Rehearsal use tracking cookies?

Only if you accept analytics. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity then load on public pages and set cookies. Neither is used in the member room.

How do I turn analytics off?

Select Cookie settings at the bottom of a public page and press Reject.

What is stored in my browser when I sign in?

A sign-in session in local storage, which is needed to keep you signed in and is removed when you sign out.
