Speaking guides for founders.

Short, practical guides you can use today. Each one ends with a drill you can do in under ten minutes.

Guide

How to stop saying um

Fillers like um, like and basically are your brain buying time. Here is a five-step way to replace them with a calm pause, with a drill you can do in five minutes.

Guide

Pitch practice for founders

Practise your pitch out loud, in short sections, and listen back. A step-by-step method for founders to rehearse an investor pitch alone, with a timer and a recording.

Guide

Public speaking for shy founders

Shy founders can build speaking confidence in small steps, starting in private. A practical ladder from reading aloud alone to speaking on camera or on stage.

Guide

Explain your startup in 60 seconds

A simple structure for a clear 60-second startup pitch: who it is for, the problem, what you do, proof and the ask. Includes a fill-in template and an example.

Guide

Speak slower and more clearly

Clear speech comes from pace, breath, finished word endings and stress on the right words. Practical exercises for founders, including tips for second-language speakers.

Guide

Answer hard investor questions

Use agree, ask, answer to handle tough investor questions calmly, and a four-part structure to keep every answer short. Includes questions to practise and what to say when you do not know.
