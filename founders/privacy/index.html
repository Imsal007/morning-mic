The speech checker in your browser

Scored practice uses the speech recognition built into your browser. Your browser may send your audio to its own provider, for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari, to turn it into words. This can happen on any take in a scored module, including takes you do not keep. That provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. The words are used in your browser to work out your scores; we do not save them.

No AI coach is connected inside Founder Rehearsal. If you press “Copy take for Claude”, text about that take, including the words the speech checker heard, is copied to your clipboard. It only goes anywhere if you paste it, and the service you paste it into then handles it under its own terms.

Who can see your data

Other members cannot see or hear your recordings, scores or profile. Authorised people at SAL Digital Solutions Ltd have technical access to the systems that store your data, and read applications, profiles and progress to build your modules. We will not play your recordings unless you ask us to, or we need to for a security or legal reason.

We do not sell your personal data. Companies that help us run the service process it for us or as part of the service they provide:

Data outside the UK

Some of these companies are based outside the UK or process data in other countries, even where our database is in London. Where UK data protection law applies to such a transfer, it needs a recognised safeguard, such as a UK adequacy decision or approved contract terms. Each provider explains its arrangements in the privacy information linked above. Email us if you want help finding the safeguard that applies.

How long we keep it

  • Applications that do not lead to membership: up to 12 months after you send them, then we delete them, including our alert email. Ask sooner and we will delete them sooner.
  • Account, profile, modules, scores and saved recordings: until you delete them or your account, or ask us to.
  • Emails: for as long as needed to deal with the matter, and normally no longer than 12 months after it ends, unless we need them for a legal reason.
  • Complaints and data requests: up to 3 years, so we can show how we handled them.
  • Website statistics: in Google Analytics, data linked to your cookie is set to be kept for 2 months. Combined statistics that do not identify you may be kept longer. Our Google Analytics cookies are set to expire after 60 days without a further visit.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Microsoft says recordings are kept for 30 days. Some, including a random sample and any marked as favourites, are kept for up to 9 months.
  • Technical records and provider backups: for the periods set by the providers above. Deleted data may remain in these for a limited time.

Deleting your data yourself

  • One recording or score: press Delete beside it on the practice page.
  • Everything in your account: open My profile, then “Delete my account and data”. This removes your account, profile, modules, scores and saved recordings.
  • Your application and emails are held separately and are not removed by that button. Email hello@founderrehearsal.com and we will delete them, unless we have a legal reason to keep something.

Training improvements and publicity

We do not currently use your voice recordings to train AI or to improve our training material, and we do not use them in adverts or as testimonials. If we ever want to, we will ask you separately and clearly. Saying no will not affect your practice. Any before-and-after clip would need your approval for that clip, we will not show your face unless you agree to it, and you can withdraw permission for future use. Remember that people may recognise you by your voice alone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy you can take elsewhere. Some rights depend on the situation. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time: delete your recordings or your account, use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of a public page to reject analytics, or email us. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before.

Email hello@founderrehearsal.com. We will answer within one month, unless the law allows longer, in which case we will tell you. We may need to check that it is really you.

Practice scoring is automatic. It only decides whether a drill is ticked and a practice module can be unlocked. It is not used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

How to make a data protection complaint

If you think we have handled your personal data wrongly, email hello@founderrehearsal.com with the subject “Data protection complaint”, or write to the address above. Tell us what happened and what you would like us to do.

  1. We will confirm we have your complaint within 30 days.
  2. We will look into it without undue delay and keep you informed.
  3. We will tell you the outcome.

You can also complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. If you live outside the UK, you may also be able to complain to your local regulator.

Age, security, cookies and changes

Founder Rehearsal is for people aged 18 or over. We use protections including encrypted connections, private storage and rules that limit each member to their own records. No online service can promise complete security.

See cookies and browser storage and our terms of use. If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell members about important changes.

Common questions

Can other members hear my recordings?

No. Other members cannot see or hear your recordings, scores or profile. Authorised people at SAL Digital Solutions Ltd have technical access to the storage, and will not play your recordings unless you ask or there is a security or legal reason.

Does Founder Rehearsal sell my data?

No. We do not sell personal data. Companies that help run the service, such as our hosting, database and sign-in providers, process it.

How do I delete my recordings?

Press Delete beside a recording, or open My profile and delete your account and data. To delete an application or emails, email hello@founderrehearsal.com.

Will my voice be used in adverts or to train AI?

Not currently. If we ever want to use a recording that way, we will ask you separately, and saying no will not affect your practice.
